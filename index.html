App privacy and support information.

Privacy

Your drawings, album, puzzle photos and progress are stored on your device. The app does not send them to our servers. Uninstalling the app may delete this data.

Photos and Camera

For My Puzzles, you choose an existing photo or take one using the camera. Permissions are requested only when you use these actions. Photos remain on your device. Up to six photos are stored; adding another replaces the oldest. You can delete photos.

Purchases

Apple handles one-time purchases and restoration of the full iOS version. The app verifies purchase ownership. The app does not receive your payment card details. Purchases can be restored using the same Apple Account.

Advertising and Tracking

The app contains no advertising, analytics or user tracking. No app account is required.

Support

A support email address will be added before the app is released. Support emails contain only the information you choose to send. You do not need to send private photos or payment details.
